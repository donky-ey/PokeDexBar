Your AI coding tokens, hatched into Pokémon.

Pick a starter, then spend the tokens you're already burning on egg draws — and hatch a collection in your macOS menu bar. Underneath it, a precise usage tracker.

or download the .zip from Releases — macOS 14 (Sonoma) or later.

The loop

Tokens in, Pokémon out

No extra command to run and nothing to click while you work. You code, the currency accrues, and the eggs hatch on their own clock.

01

Pick a starter

On first launch, choose 1 of 27 first-stage Pokémon spanning Gen 1–9. Never shiny — it's your partner.

02

Code as usual

Every token you burn in a supported CLI becomes spendable currency and feeds your partner's experience at the same time.

03

Draw an egg

Spend that currency in the Shop for an egg of a randomly rolled grade — Common 60% / Rare 22% / Epic 15% / Legendary 3%. A short reveal plays first — one burst of colour per grade, from a single white flash up to four bursts ending in sparkling orange for a Legendary. Three incubate at once, six with a slot upgrade.

04

Hatch on real time

30 minutes for a Common up to 24 hours for a Legendary — wall-clock time, counting down even while the app is closed, with a notification once it's ready. A ripe egg waits in its slot, cracked, until you tap Open — then it rocks, bursts, and the Pokémon springs out of it. Species with a regional look have a 20% chance to hatch Alolan, Galarian, Hisuian or Paldean.

05

Evolve by hand

Pokémon level from 1 to 100 on the games' own six growth curves, and evolve at the games' own levels — a Charmander becomes a Charmeleon at 16. Code, or feed EXP Candy, then tap to evolve once it's ready. Branching lines let you pick the path. A level alone isn't always enough: 56 branches want a stone, 25 want the item a trade would have held — and none of it is for sale. A partner wearing a Ribbon brings back the item it needs.

06

Fill a Dex and a Box

The National Dex tracks every species you've hatched, #1 to #1025, silhouettes for the rest. Your Box is a grid of every individual you own — tap a cell for a detail screen where you set your partner, feed candy, evolve, or reshape it with a Mega Stone or Max Mushroom.

✨ Once in a rare while — Shiny

Shiny hatches keep their distinct colors everywhere — menu bar, home card, Box — through every evolution. Base odds are 1/64, or 1/48 with the Shiny Charm.

Underneath

A usage tracker you'll actually open

The game sits on top of a precise reading of your local logs — no dashboard, no browser tab.

Today

Spend and its API equivalent at a glance

Today's total tokens sit in the menu bar, compact (e.g. 200.7M). Add today's API equivalent or a limit percentage — or turn it all off for a character-only bar. Per-service tabs break it down in the popover.

Limits

Official 5-hour and weekly windows

Claude and Codex utilization with live reset countdowns, right under today's numbers, colored as you approach the cap.

Forecast

Burn rate, projected

Projects when the current 5-hour window will hit 100% at your present pace, so a wall never surprises you.

Works with

Seven CLIs, read straight from disk

Claude Code Codex Gemini CLI OpenCode Hermes Agent Cursor Grok CLI

All of them are read from local log and database files on your Mac. There is no external usage CLI to install and no account to connect — install the app and whichever tools you already use show up. Your usage is never uploaded, and the app never runs a model turn of its own.

Install

One command, about a minute

Homebrew

The recommended way

The app is ad-hoc / self-signed, and the cask strips the quarantine attribute for you — nothing else to do.

Direct download

Without Homebrew

⬇ PokeDexBar.zip

Unzip it and drag PokeDexBar.app into /Applications. On first launch, right-click it → Open, or run xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine /Applications/PokeDexBar.app once.

Requires macOS 14 (Sonoma) or later, Apple Silicon or Intel. First release: v1.0.0. Prefer to build it yourself? swift build · swift test · ./scripts/build-app.sh.
